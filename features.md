## Core Features

### 1. Form Customization
- **Fully customizable COD order forms** with drag-and-drop editor
- **Popup or embedded deployment options** - flexibility in how the form appears on your store
- **Custom fields, buttons, messages, and layouts** - tailor the form to match your brand
- **Address validation and Google Autocomplete integration** - helps customers fill out forms faster and more accurately
